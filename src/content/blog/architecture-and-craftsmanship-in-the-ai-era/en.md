Sunday evening thought: work on your software architecture and craftsmanship.

It has never mattered more than in the AI era... and whatever you do, don't listen to anyone who says otherwise.

## A vocabulary worth mastering

- [Transaction Script](https://www.martinfowler.com/eaaCatalog/transactionScript.html)
- [Active Record](https://www.martinfowler.com/eaaCatalog/activeRecord.html)
- [DDD](https://www.domainlanguage.com/ddd/blue-book/) (Domain-Driven Design)
- [Event Sourcing](https://www.martinfowler.com/eaaDev/EventSourcing.html)
- [Clean Architecture](/blog/what-is-clean-architecture/)
- [CQRS](https://www.martinfowler.com/bliki/CQRS.html)
- [Vertical Slice Architecture](/blog/vertical-slice-architecture/)
- [BDD](https://dannorth.net/blog/introducing-bdd/) (Behavior-Driven Development)
- [TDD](https://www.informit.com/store/test-driven-development-by-example-9780321146533) (Test-Driven Development)
- [SOLID](https://objectmentor.com/resources/articles/Principles_and_Patterns.pdf)
- [BBOM](https://www.laputan.org/mud/mud.html) (that one, avoid it, haha)

... and so on.

Knowing when to choose what, for which reasons, and mastering it all.

## More accessible than ever

On top of that, it's absolutely fascinating, and nowadays super accessible with the internet and a handful of books.

If you don't know where to start, my first recommendation is [Learning DDD](https://vladikk.com/page/books/) by Khononov (DDD-focused, but you have to start somewhere).

To go further on the Model question in DDD, I've also written about [the difference between an ORM's Model and the domain model](/blog/model-orm-vs-domain-model/).
