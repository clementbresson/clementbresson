> "With AI, we don't learn anything anymore. Juniors will never get experience again."

I have a bit of a problem with this idea of the moment.

For those doing full vibe coding without understanding anything... it's true.

But for those who **really** want to learn? AI is an incredible catalyst!

There was oral tradition.
Then books.
Then the internet, its forums, its YouTube tutorials...
...and now AI.

Used with a self-taught mindset, it's insane.

You can ask it for explanations in **your** context, have it make you interactive diagrams, complete examples, tailor-made exercises... the possibilities are endless.

## That's where all the difference lies

- Ask it to do it for you => you learn nothing.
- Ask it to teach you how to do it => you learn faster than ever.

Provided you put in [a bit of effort](/blog/learning-to-learn-efficiently/). But that has never changed :)

It's also what I encourage in [the developers I coach](/work-with-me/).

Don't let those who say otherwise discourage you.

Happy learning, and have a great weekend!
