What is this new trend of:

> "Full remote freelance, but [CITY] or [COUNTRY] only"?

It makes absolutely no sense.

At a stretch, saying:

- French/European registered company only
- French working hours only

That can make sense from a legal and organisational standpoint...

...but requiring someone to be physically somewhere for a truly full remote job is absurd.

I [freelance fully remote](/work-with-me/) myself.

Curious to hear your views (especially from recruiters).

PS: it's a shame for recruiters, you are one red flag away (one that makes no sense?) from really attracting [top talent](/blog/full-remote-freelance-only/).
