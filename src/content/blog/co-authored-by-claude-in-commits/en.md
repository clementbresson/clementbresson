[Do you own](/blog/co-authored-by-claude-debate/) the `Co-Authored-By: Claude` lines in your commits and PRs?

Me... 100% :)

Working flat out with AI and trying to hide every trace of it makes no sense today.

The job is changing, and that's normal.

## Co-authored does not mean "eyes closed"

And careful: [co-authored](https://docs.github.com/en/pull-requests/how-tos/commit-changes/creating-a-commit-with-multiple-authors) does not mean "[Claude](https://claude.com/product/overview) did everything and I accepted it with my eyes closed". The commit remains **my** responsibility, even if I did not type the lines by hand.

That holds on my own projects as much as [on the ones where I come in to lend a hand](/work-with-me/).

## Not seeing it is suspicious

I would almost go as far as saying that **not** seeing any co-authored is suspicious.

Either AI isn't being used (in 2026... not a good sign), or it's hypocrisy (not much better).
