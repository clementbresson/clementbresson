Thanks to AI, more and more small businesses are [building their own internal tools](/blog/the-developer-market-is-shifting/). But many of them feel that:

- it is getting out of control
- they have no idea what is underneath it
- they do not know whether it is secure (it generally is not)
- one thing breaks every time they change another
- they are afraid to touch anything
- they would like to distribute it and charge for it, but do not know how to go about it
- etc.

If this sounds like you, here is how I can help.

I now exclusively offer support to small businesses where [tech is a support function, not the core business](/blog/the-part-time-senior-developer/), and that build their tools in-house (often with AI).

## How my support works

1. **We take stock**: your needs, your problems, what already exists (we invent nothing, we try to answer your real problems). Half a day.
2. **I audit** the current state of what has been built: what is worth keeping or not, fixable or not (or left as is, because some things are already very good).
3. **We plan** a progressive clean-up and tackle it over time, targeting the highest priority first.
4. **As long as everything is not solid**, we keep going, and then I help you keep up the pace over time, at a rate of one or two days a week.

The goal: that you need me as little as possible to move forward. I am there to bring my experience where it matters most: framing, architecture, security, updates, technology watch... while you focus on your own business.

Everyone wins: I cost you [20% of a full-time senior's price](/blog/daily-rates-after-ai/). You gain 100% peace of mind.

Careful, I am not promising the moon. One or two days a week are not enough to build and maintain Facebook or [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/). But you will get simple, right-sized, secure tools, followed by someone who knows how they work... and knows how they need to be maintained so that nothing is obsolete a year later.

I still have room for one client at two days a week, or two clients at one day a week: I would be delighted to work with you (and in the worst case, just to talk tech and projects!).
