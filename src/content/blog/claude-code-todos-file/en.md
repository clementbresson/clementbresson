In the middle of a feature, you often stumble on an unrelated detail: a warning, a pattern that isn't followed, a bit of debt to settle. The usual options: ignore it, fix it on the way (risky if it's complex), or remember it (optimistic).

The best option: write it down.

The idea: one very simple instruction in your [CLAUDE.md](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/memory): any detail spotted during a task must be noted, concisely, in a `TODOS.md` file. You can also trigger it by hand with a `/btw to do later: ...` in [Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code).

The result: between two tasks, you have a memo of [small things to clean up](/blog/todos-and-human-todos-files/). And in the AI era, a clean, strict codebase is still the [best harness you can get](/blog/rigorous-codebase-best-ai-harness/).

I add this rule to [the CLAUDE.md of every project I take on](/work-with-me/).

PS: this is not a replacement for [Linear](https://linear.app/) or anything else. Just a space for what doesn't deserve a ticket, a quick memo so you don't forget.
