For those of you using [Orca](https://orca.dev/): a nice update today!

All conversations with Claude (and probably the other providers) now have real formatting.

I was already often generating artifacts or Markdown + preview to make things easier to read... but a good part of my day is still spent in the terminal, talking to [my orchestrator](/blog/my-agentic-workflow-parallel-worktrees/).

And honestly... having something that looks halfway decent will do wonders for the eyes.

## A real need always ends up native

I know many people had built overlays to handle this... but now, at least in Orca, it is native.

And that is why, I think, spending too much time building your own overlays is often a waste of time. Everything moves very, very fast, and as soon as a real need exists, it ends up becoming native.

Homemade orchestrator, overlays, etc.: everything gets overtaken quickly by people who do **only** that.

## Invest in what lasts

Better to keep that time to sharpen [deep, lasting knowledge](/blog/ai-fatigue-back-to-fundamentals/) of software engineering (patterns, architecture, DDD, etc.). That is also the foundation [I bring to the teams I work with](/work-with-me/).

Happy orchestrating!
