Quick idea of the day, for when you have a feature finished locally and want the stakeholders to sign it off.

Ask [Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/claude) to:

1. prepare a complete test scenario for the feature in the browser;
2. play it from start to finish on a [local instance](/blog/fresh-local-instance-and-observability-for-ai-agents/)...
3. ...recording the whole thing along the way, with captions!

The idea matters more than the tooling: there are plenty of ways to handle the frames + encoding part.

But the result is the same: a lightweight video of the feature, end to end.

I used to do this by hand, talking in front of my screen. It worked, but it was a chore (on a slightly longer demo, you have to rehearse and prepare your script)... Now it is spot on and efficient, straight away.

This is now how [I show a finished feature to my clients' stakeholders](/work-with-me/).

You're welcome! :)
