A technique I love with AI: at the root of the codebase, I keep a `todos` markdown file and a `human-todos` markdown file.

Then I ask the AI to [note down everything it spots as development goes along](/blog/claude-code-todos-file/) (there are always little things). It keeps a list for me without pulling my focus from the task at hand.

The `human-todos` is for what lives outside the code, what only I can do (or an agent I hand control of the browser to...).

And regularly, I start 1 or 2 background sessions to kill the `todos`.

The result: I don't drift during a session, while tackling technical debt and small improvements on the side. It's one of the habits [I pass on to the teams I help](/work-with-me/).

Seasoned readers will tell me all of this could very easily go into [Linear](https://linear.app/) tickets through an [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/)... and they'd be right :)

But I like my `todos`! And when I see it grow, it makes my hair stand on end and I notice it right away (vs a backlog which, as we all know, is just a ticket graveyard).
