**The** useful dev skill **nobody** talks about?

Knowing how to learn efficiently.

Why?

There are 300 posts a day on LinkedIn explaining, rightly, that with AI the value of a tech person now lies in [architecture](/blog/architecture-and-craftsmanship-in-the-ai-era/), [understanding the business](/blog/the-value-is-in-the-business-rules/), mastery...

... But all of these rest on solid knowledge...

... which has to be learned!

And faster than ever.

So rather than:

- skimming
- thinking that reading = understanding (the worst)
- staying passive in front of AI
- ... etc.

Try to:

- rephrase in your head or out loud
- write a summary
- summarise a book you've read for yourself
- cross-check concepts in your mind
- take a break
- ... etc.

So resist the urge to move at AI's frantic pace... take your time, and learn properly.

Over time, it pays off.

That's what I make myself do for [each new business domain I have to learn for a client](/work-with-me/).

Happy learning!

PS: unfortunately, too few people really learned how to learn at school... but it's never too late!
