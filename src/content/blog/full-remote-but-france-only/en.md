Recruiters and clients... someone is going to have to explain this to me.

I see more and more job ads saying "Full Remote"... with a little "France" added after it.

Why?

## Reasons that can make sense

For some projects, at a stretch, the following reasons can make sense:

- a contract under French law only;
- working hours aligned with the French time zone;
- an on-site seminar every X months;
- a "good working environment" required;
- and so on.

For legal or organisational reasons, you would rather work within the French legal framework, and not with someone who takes every call from a beach café with no internet.

## But why require a country?

But if it is full remote, **real** full remote... why require a country? What's the problem?

If there is a lawyer in the room, I would love the answer (careful, we are talking about freelancing here, not permanent contracts).

Because it is a real shame.

By advertising "full remote", you had done 90% of the work to attract the best profiles (the ones who have a choice)...

...and you shoot yourself in the foot with the very next word.

For me and many freelancers, it is an instant red flag: you can tell something is off behind that little addition.

## The best are everywhere

I'll say it again: [the day I hire](/blog/full-remote-freelance-only/) (as soon as possible, I hope), I will look for freelancers who are full remote **worldwide**. Because that is where the best are.

And in return, I will expect results... whatever the method and organisation that gets there. That is also [what I bring to my clients](/work-with-me/).

I just don't understand why so few people get it.

- Control freak?
- Jealousy?
- Something else?

It is as if companies were looking for everything except what matters most:

- the best profiles;
- autonomy;
- results.
