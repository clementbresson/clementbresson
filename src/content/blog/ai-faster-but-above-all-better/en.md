With AI we go faster, yes.

But above all: we can do **better**.

That's where the game is played. And I see too few people talking about it.

Average projects will drown in a mass of average projects.

But those who use AI to [raise the bar](/blog/ai-time-savings-quantity-or-quality/) (quality, innovation) will go far. Very far. That's the direction [I take the teams I work alongside](/work-with-me/).

When competition explodes, the bar goes up.
