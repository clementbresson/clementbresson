A [Stanford study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.03622) had 2 groups write code: with AI, and without.

The group with AI:

- wrote significantly less secure code;
- **and** was more confident in the security of their code.

Why? When you ask the AI to evaluate what it just did… it often says everything is spotless.

But open a conversation with a fresh context (no ego :) and ask it to evaluate, to find flaws in what another AI just did…

… it will find problems, guaranteed!

And while it sometimes overdoes it, sometimes it can also give you cold sweats…

In short: stay in control! You are the boss.

PS: the study was run on 47 **real** devs. Imagine the score with someone who does not read the code at all :)
