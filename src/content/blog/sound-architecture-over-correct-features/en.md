Sound architecture with poorly built features… you'll survive.

Shaky architecture with correct features… you might survive, but everything will feel like a fight.

And unfortunately, architecture is the most complicated thing in software engineering:

- it depends on the context;
- it depends on what the team knows;
- it can, and should, evolve over time with the stage of the project;
- there is an infinite number of possible combinations, with a huge number of trade-offs to make;
- …

Fellow vibe coders, get support on what matters most. [A short audit](/work-with-me/), maybe a few days to set up the structure, then a few regular check-ups: your projects will thank you in the long run!
