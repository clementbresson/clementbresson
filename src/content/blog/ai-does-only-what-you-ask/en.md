AI is a wonderful tool that does exactly what you tell it…

… and only what you tell it.

Someone for whom this isn't their job rarely asks it:

- to check security
- to stay consistent with the existing code
- to take this or that upcoming need into account
- to compare 5 architecture options to meet the need
- … etc.

The result? Something that works and looks perfect on the surface…

… but is riddled with problems!

Asking those questions is [precisely my job](/work-with-me/).
