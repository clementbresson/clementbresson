Among developers, 9 side projects out of 10 die right after launch, for a very simple reason.

Once the product's MVP is done (the fun part), everything else gets neglected:

- **operations**: back office, notifications, transactional emails…
- **observability**: logs, traces, metrics, alerts…
- **sales**: payment, invoicing…
- **customer service**: support, FAQ…
- **marketing**: consent, email sequences…
- **maintenance**: CI/CD, tests, updates, versioning…

All of these are less sexy, but absolutely essential if you want to go far.

And I am not even talking about [the business side](/blog/distribution-is-the-hard-part/): market research, market fit, potential pivots, strategy, competitive watch, communication, sales, partnerships, prospecting…

In short: the product core is (at best) 50% of the tech, which is itself (at best) 50% of the project.

So instead of doing 25% of 8 projects in a row, why not try 100% of 2?
