With AI, the frantic pace of releases to keep up with, [workflows to change](/blog/developer-workflows-are-fragmenting/), tools to try...

...developers who weren't doing JS over the last 10-15 years finally understand what JS developers went through.

Tiring, isn't it? :)

Hang in there, everyone trying to keep up! And I'm not sure it will slow down in the years ahead...

## My technique

When all the new stuff wears me out: go back and work on the [fundamentals of software engineering and architecture](/blog/architecture-and-craftsmanship-in-the-ai-era/).

That, at least, is relatively stable. And it compounds over time.

It's also what I put back on the table first [alongside the developers I support](/work-with-me/).
