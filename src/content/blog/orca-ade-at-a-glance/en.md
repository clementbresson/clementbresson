[Orca](https://www.onorca.dev/) is honestly really well designed, I think.

All my repos visible at a glance.

For each one, creating and managing worktrees in a few clicks.

In each worktree, an agent working on an isolated instance of the program (well, that part is because I pushed the DX so this would be possible across all my projects).

An overview of every agent running: the ones that finished, the ones waiting for a reply.

Nothing new in itself... but when you work with 3 to 6 sessions in parallel across 1 to 3 repositories at once, it gives you real visibility.

And... I barely open [Cursor](https://cursor.com/) anymore.

Do you use an [ADE](/blog/developer-workflows-are-fragmenting/) too? Have you tried others?
